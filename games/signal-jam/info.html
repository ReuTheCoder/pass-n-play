Signal Jam

Game Information

Players

4 - 10

Time

10-15 minutes

Difficulty

Easy

Description

Work together to identify a hidden Signal using quick "20 Questions" style inquires before time runs out. Be careful: one player is the Jammer, secretly trying to interfere with the signal mislead the team.

Inspired by Bézier Games’ Werewords.

How to Play

Roles

  • The Operator — 1 player. They reveal their role immediately after receiving the Operator role card. They know the secret Signal and control the response system.
  • The Jammer — 1 player. They know the secret Signal and secretly try to prevent the group from decoding it.
  • The Decoder — 1 player. They know the secret Signal and secretly try to guide the group toward the correct answer without revealing their role.
  • The Listeners — All remianing players. They do not know the secret Signal and must work together to decode it.

Objective

The Listeners must identify the secret Signal before the timer runs out. The secret signal is a single secret word that is always a noun. The Operator knows the Signal and responds to the group's questions, while the Decoder and Jammer secretly influence the discussion.

Transmission Phase

  • Players ask the Operator questions to gather clues about the secret Signal.
  • The Operator responds using Yes, Maybe, No, or Correct.
  • Players can discuss the Operator's responses and use them to narrow down the Signal.
  • The Decoder subtly helps guide the group toward the correct answer while the Jammer secretly tries to create confusion and waste time.
  • The group must decode the Signal before the timer reaches zero.

Final Deduction

If the group does not decode the Signal before time runs out:

  1. The group discusses who they believe the Jammer is. (The suggested discssion time is 1 minute)
  2. The players vote to identify the Jammer.
  3. If they are wrong, the game ends. If the group correctly identifies the Jammer, the Jammer gets one final chance to identify the Decoder.

If the group successfully decodes the Signal before time runs out:

  1. The Jammer reveals their role and gets one final chance to identify the Decoder.

Win Conditions

  • The Listeners and Decoder win if they decode the Signal or correctly identify the Jammer, and the Jammer fails to identify the Decoder.
  • The Jammer wins if the group fails to decode the Signal before time runs out and do not identify the Jammer.
  • The Jammer also wins if they are identified but correctly identify the Decoder during the Final Deduction.

Game Flow

  1. Choose players.
  2. Enter names.
  3. Pass the device and read your role.
  4. Begin the Transmission
  5. Ask questions and decode the Signal before time runs out.
  6. Identify the Jammer
  7. Jammer attempts to identify Decoder
