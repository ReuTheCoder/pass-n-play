🔎 Secret Saboteur

👥 Players

3 - 10

⏱ Time

5-15 minutes

Difficulty

Easy

Description

One player secretly tries to sabotage the team's guesses while blending in.
Inspired by Rabble's Bot or Not card game

How to Play

Roles

  • The Guesser — 1 player. Their role is revealed to everyone at the start of the game.
  • The Saboteur — A set number of players (based on game settings). Their role is kept secret.
  • The Teammate — All remaining players.

Objective

Everyone except the Guesser receives the same secret number from 1–10. A prompt card shows a scale from 1–10 that players must use when giving their responses.

Discussion Phase

  • Teammates give responses that accurately match the secret number on the prompt scale.
  • Saboteurs give misleading responses to confuse the Guesser.
  • The Guesser listens to everyone’s responses and tries to determine the secret number.

Guessing Phase

  1. The Guesser must first identify who they believe the Saboteur is.
  2. After naming their guess aloud, they must enter their choice on the screen.
  3. Then the Guesser guesses the secret number.

Win Conditions

  • Teammates win if the Guesser correctly identifies the Saboteur orcorrectly guesses the secret number.
  • Saboteurs win if they remain hidden andthe Guesser guesses the wrong number.

Game Flow

  1. Choose players.
  2. Enter names.
  3. Pass the device.
  4. Read your role.
  5. Discuss.
  6. Guess the number & saboteur.
