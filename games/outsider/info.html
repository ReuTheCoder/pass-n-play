The Outsider

Game Information

Players

3 - 8

Time

3 - 5 minutes

Difficulty

Easy

Description

One player, the Outsider is completely in the dark, and everyone else knows the secret word. To survive, the Outsider must discretely bluff their way through the game and figure out which word on the game board is the secret word; all while the Insiders try to sniff out the fraud.

Inspired by Big Potato Games’ The Chameleon.

How to Play

Roles

  • The Outsider — 1 player who does not know the secret word. Their goal is to figure the word out without exposing their role.
  • The Insiders — All remianing players who are given the secret word. Their goal is to identify the Outsider.

Objective

A single secret word is chosen. All Insiders know the word, while the Outsider does not. Everyone gives clues about the word while trying not to reveal too much. Try to fool the Outisder into guessing a different word on the board. The Insiders must figure out who is bluffing, while the Outsider tries to stay hidden.

Role Reveal Phase

The device is passed around the group. Each player taps to reveal their role. Insiders see the secret word, while the Outsider sees that they are the Outsider.

After viewing their role, each player must hide the card before passing the device to the next player.

Clue Phase

Players take turns giving one word related to the secret word. Insiders should be specific enough to prove they know the word, but not so obvious that the Outsider can figure it out. The Outsider must listen carefully and give a plausible clue based on the other players' answers.

Once everyone has given a clue, the group discusses the clues and decides who they think is the Outsider.

Voting Phase

All players vote on who they believe is the Outsider. The player with the most votes is identified as the group's choice.

If the chosen player is the Outsider, they must reveal their role and move on to the final guess.

Outsider's Final Guess

If the Outsider is correctly identified, they get one chance to guess the secret word. They choose their answer from the available words.

If there are only three players, the Outsider gets two guesses.

Win Conditions

  • Insiders win if they identify the Outsider and the Outsider fails to guess the secret word.
  • Outsider wins if they avoid being identified or correctly guess the secret word after being caught.

Scoring

You can play individual rounds, or keep score across multiple rounds. If keeping score:

  • Outsider: 2 points if they are not identified.
  • Outsider: 1 point if they are identified but correctly guess the secret word.
  • Insiders: 2 points each if they identify the Outsider and the Outsider guesses incorrectly.

The first player to reach 5 points wins.

Game Flow

  1. Choose players.
  2. Enter names.
  3. Read your role.
  4. Pass the device.
  5. Give one-word clues.
  6. Discuss the clues.
  7. Vote for the Outsider.
  8. Let the Outsider make their final guess.
  9. Reveal the results.
