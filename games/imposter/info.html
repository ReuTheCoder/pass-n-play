Imposter

Game Information

Players

3 - 10

Time

5-15 minutes

Difficulty

Easy

Description

A social deduction word game where players give clues to a secret word while one player (the imposter) tries to blend in without knowing it.

Through bluffing and guessing, the imposter must give clues that sound plausible to avoid being discovered, while the other players try to identify who is faking it.

Inspired by Imposter Who?

How to Play

Roles

  • The Imposter — A set number of players (based on game settings). Their role is kept secret.
  • The Player — All remaining players who know the secret word.

Objective

A single secret word is chosen. All Players know the word. The Imposters do not. Players must identify the Imposters before the Imposters blend in successfully.

Discussion Phase

Players take turns saying one word related to the secret word to prove they know it. Imposters must listen carefully and use the Players’ clues to avoid being discovered. Players may discuss, question each other, and analyze clues as the round continues.

You can decide:

  • a time limit, or
  • a fixed number of clue rounds

Once the discussion ends, everyone moves to voting.

Voting Phase

All players vote on who they believe the Imposter is. Majority rules. If the chosen player is an Imposter, they must reveal their role.

Win Conditions

  • Imposters win if they survive undetected until the end of the round.
  • Players win if they correctly identify all Imposters.

Game Flow

  1. Choose players.
  2. Enter names.
  3. Read your role.
  4. Pass the device.
  5. Discuss.
  6. Vote the imposter.
